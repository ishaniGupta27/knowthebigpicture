Create a visual explanation of HOW something works or is made.

Silently identify the smallest meaningful transformation:

starting point -> major action -> important change -> finished result

Include only steps that change what the viewer understands or can recognize.
Explain what changes at each step using familiar verbs such as cut, crushed,
heated, mixed, dried, filtered, aged, or carried. Omit invisible specialist detail
unless it is the central answer. Do not make every minor production step a slide.

Use 5-6 slides total, including the opening question slide.

### Opening hook (narration, question slide)
Point at something they use or see, then tease the hidden process behind it. Keep
it to 6-12 words; skip the wind-up.
Openers (punchy): "You use X daily. Here's how it's really made.", "X works.
But how, exactly?"
