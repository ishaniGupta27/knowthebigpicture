Create a discovery-led collection of distinct, recognizable varieties.

Silently define one organizing principle before selecting items. Every item must
belong at the same conceptual level. Do not mix types, subtypes, brands,
ingredients, preparation methods, and broad families.

The first slide is the exact input and uses role `question`. Every remaining slide
uses role `type`, names exactly one variety in the heading, and gives its single
most memorable difference in the explanation.

Choose items that are meaningfully different, visually recognizable, and
explainable in one familiar sentence. Do not give a definition, origin story,
ingredient list, and usage note for every item. Pick the one distinction a viewer
will remember. Do not repeat the same distinction across items.

Return exactly the requested number of type slides plus the opening slide. Treat
the collection as selected popular or useful examples, not an exhaustive taxonomy.
All slides are priority 1.

### Opening hook (narration, question slide)
Tease the range they are about to discover; use the count when it lands.
Openers: "How many kinds of X do you actually know?", "There are more types of X than you'd think."
