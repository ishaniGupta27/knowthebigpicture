Create a plain-language introduction to something viewers may recognize but not
fully understand.

Use this path when useful:

plain description -> what it does -> familiar example -> why people care ->
important misconception or takeaway

Do not produce a dictionary entry or technical inventory. Describe what the
subject does before naming how specialists classify it. Answer "why should I
care?" as well as "what is it?"

Use 5-6 slides total, including the opening question slide.
